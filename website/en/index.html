Evidence before explanation

Public research covers trading Binance USDT perpetuals only: relative BTC moves, cross-sectional turnover and funding deviations. Closed-hour prices, open interest, settled funding and visible depth retain units, timestamps and gaps. Partial depth is a lower bound, not fabricated liquidity.

AI research is explicit-action only: at most two model calls, 1,200 output tokens each and a 75-second overall budget. Numeric references and conditions are checked; missing evidence becomes observation. Valid references do not prove semantic correctness. Costs and actual usage are unknown.

Follow, observe, review

Following is separate from alerts. Price and funding crossings are checked every 60 seconds while the app is running and online, producing an in-app alert once. Sleep or exit gaps are not backfilled. Original judgments, failures and abstentions are frozen. Optional forward price observations use the next fresh last-traded price; fees, funding, slippage and net returns remain unknown.

Your data and controls

Settings and keys use asynchronous macOS system encryption, without plaintext fallback or old-preview migration. Public research records use an atomic local JSON file with 0600 permissions, not encryption; user-selected watch and alert rules may reveal preferences. Public AI research sends no account positions or equity. Separate portfolio analysis is opt-in and does send sensitive position risk facts to the chosen provider.

The app defaults to a light theme and green-up/red-down colors, with a reverse option. Hidden amounts neutralize the native menu signal. Updates are checked on request, downloaded in the app, verified, and installed on restart or the next quit once ready. Version 0.2.2 and earlier require one manual installation to gain this capability. No orders, transfers, performance guarantees or exchange affiliation.
